Pay Parking indplate OCR model
Base model: Tesseract tessdata_best English LSTM.
Upstream: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best
License: Apache License, Version 2.0. See LICENSE.

Modified for Pay Parking: fine-tuned on generated Indian state and Bharat-series
registration text rendered in Nimbus Sans, Liberation Sans and DejaVu Sans Mono.
The fonts approximate plate lettering. No official font or street images were
included. The model was quantized and general-purpose dictionaries removed.
